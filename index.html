Enter the Hollow

The legends are closer than they seem.

In one quiet West Virginia hollow, old stories are part of everyday life. But when strange visitors, forgotten places, and creatures from Appalachian legend begin crossing into the lives of three siblings, the Darnell family discovers that the mountains have kept more than memories.

Volume One brings together three connected adventures that deepen the mystery while pulling Charlotte, Sylvia, and Rance toward a calling none of them expected.

About the Author

Randy Darnell

Randy Darnell is an educator, pastor, and author whose storytelling draws on West Virginia history, Appalachian folklore, faith, and family. Guardians of the Hollow brings those threads together in an adventure built around ordinary people facing extraordinary mysteries.

The world of the Hollow is only beginning. Future stories will continue exploring the legends, places, and secrets waiting beyond the familiar mountain roads.
