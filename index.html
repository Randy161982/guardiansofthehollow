Some legends should never be awakened.

Charlotte, Sylvia, and Rance have grown up with stories of strange creatures and forgotten mysteries in the West Virginia mountains. Enter the Hollow

Every legend leaves a shadow.

The Darnell children live in a secluded Appalachian hollow where family stories, old artifacts, abandoned places, and West Virginia folklore begin to collide. Volume One follows the siblings through three connected supernatural adventures as the meaning of the word Guardian becomes increasingly personal.

Volume One

Three stories. One family. A hidden calling.

Set in 1950s West Virginia, Guardians of the Hollow blends Appalachian folklore, supernatural mystery, family adventure, and faith. Charlotte, Sylvia, and Rance begin by searching for legends. Before long, the legends are searching for them.

About the Author

Randy Darnell

Randy Darnell is an educator, pastor, and author whose storytelling draws on West Virginia history, Appalachian folklore, faith, family, and the enduring appeal of a good mystery. Guardians of the Hollow is the beginning of a larger world in which familiar mountain legends may be only the first glimpse of something much older.
